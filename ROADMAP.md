# Product roadmap

## Next candidates

### Save transcript

Allow the designer to explicitly save the complete meeting transcript for later review.

Product constraints:

- Opt-in only; never archive sessions automatically.
- Save text and session metadata, not the selected frame.
- Clearly preview what will be saved before writing anything.
- Prefer a local text or Markdown export before adding accounts or cloud history.
- Include design intent, dialogue, outcome, timestamp, and final hidden-state summary.

### Save SURVIVED artifact

Allow the designer to save or share the final SURVIVED report without saving the full transcript.

Product constraints:

- Export the report as a polished standalone artifact.
- Exclude the selected frame and conversation by default.
- Include metric movement, outcome, what changed, momentum, boss contribution, unresolved concern, assessment, and closing line.
- Explore PNG and Markdown first; consider clipboard and PDF later.
- Make the artifact understandable without exposing private design work.

## Still intentionally deferred

- Automatic cloud archives
- Accounts and payments
- Team analytics
- Figma comments
- Multiple boss personas
